Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Open the appointments page, complete the request form and contact the real hospital to confirm availability. This demo does not create a real booking.

Are the doctors listed on this website verified?

No. The sample profiles are illustrative and must be replaced with verified clinicians, qualifications and registrations.

How can I confirm a facility or service?

Use the official hospital contact details to confirm that the service is offered, its operating hours, preparation requirements and any fees.

Does this form send an email?

No. It demonstrates browser-side validation only. A backend and secure email or booking service are required for real submissions.
