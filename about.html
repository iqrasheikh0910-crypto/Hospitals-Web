MediNova is a healthcare website concept built around empathy, clear information and a more welcoming patient journey.

Who we are

Healthcare should feel human.

Every patient's journey is different. Our concept places clear communication, respectful care and accessible service information at the centre of the hospital experience.

From learning about a specialty to requesting a consultation, the aim is to help patients make informed next steps. Actual services, staffing and clinical credentials must be verified by the operating hospital.

Our mission, vision & values

The principles behind the MediNova concept.

Our mission

Make healthcare information easier to understand and help people connect with appropriate medical services.

Our vision

Create a welcoming, accessible and patient-centred digital experience across every stage of care.

Our values

Respect, privacy, transparency, accessibility and responsible communication.
