Department guide

What to expect

Cardiology

Cardiologists evaluate heart and circulation concerns, review relevant history and may recommend tests based on clinical assessment.

  • Bring current medicines and relevant previous reports.
  • Ask about prevention, follow-up and any recommended tests.

Neurology

Neurology consultations may address recurring headaches, nerve symptoms, seizures and other neurological concerns.

  • Bring a record of symptoms and previous investigations.
  • Sudden weakness, facial drooping or difficulty speaking needs emergency help.

Pediatrics

Child health visits can include age-appropriate assessment, growth discussions and preventive care.

  • Bring vaccination and medical records if available.
  • For a critically ill child, seek emergency medical care.

Gynecology

Women's health clinicians assess reproductive health concerns and discuss appropriate testing and care options.

  • Share relevant medical history and medicines.
  • Severe bleeding or severe pain may require urgent assessment.

Orthopedics

Orthopedic care focuses on bones, joints, ligaments and mobility, including injury assessment.

  • Bring previous imaging and reports.
  • Suspected serious fractures require urgent medical attention.

Dermatology

Skin specialists assess skin, hair and nail changes and recommend treatment after evaluation.

  • Note when symptoms started and products or medicines used.
  • Rapid swelling or breathing difficulty is an emergency.

Dentistry

Dental consultations support oral health assessment, prevention and treatment planning.

  • Share allergies and relevant medication history.
  • Facial swelling with breathing or swallowing difficulty is an emergency.

Radiology

Imaging availability and preparation depend on the requested test and clinical indication.

  • Follow the facility's specific preparation instructions.
  • Tell staff if pregnancy is possible before relevant imaging.

Laboratory

Testing options, sample requirements and turnaround times vary by test.

  • Ask whether fasting or other preparation is required.
  • Results should be interpreted with a qualified clinician.

General Medicine

General medicine provides assessment for common health concerns and coordination with specialists when appropriate.

General Surgery

Surgical consultations explain assessment, potential options, preparation and follow-up. Decisions require an in-person clinical evaluation.
