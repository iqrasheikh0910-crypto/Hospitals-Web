Know before you arrive.

Facility arrangements differ between hospitals. A quick confirmation can help you prepare and avoid unnecessary delays.

  • Confirm the service and opening hours.
  • Ask whether an appointment or referral is needed.
  • Check required documents and test preparation.
  • Ask about accessibility, fees and payment options.
